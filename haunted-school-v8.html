HAUNTED SCHOOL

Midnight. The doors are locked. Something tall in a striped hat walks these halls, and it hunts by sound. You can't see it from afar, but you'll hear it.

Open the classroom doors and search the drawers and filing cabinets (look down at them, press E) to find 5 keycards, then escape through the green doors at the west end of the main hall. Grab batteries to keep your light alive. Turn the light off to hide in the dark.
NEVER open a red-handled door. Sprinting is loud.
